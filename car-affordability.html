HOW IT WORKS

How the car affordability calculator works

1. Monthly income

Enter your monthly gross income. If you are planning the purchase together with your household finances, you can use the income amount that is relevant to your own loan planning.

2. Existing commitments

Include your existing monthly debt commitments such as housing loans, personal loans, student loans and other regular financing payments.

3. Target DSR

The debt-service ratio determines how much of your income is allocated to debt payments. A lower target DSR gives you a more conservative car budget.

4. Car financing

The calculator estimates the loan amount using your selected interest rate, down payment and loan tenure.

5. Monthly instalment

Your estimated monthly instalment is compared with your income and existing commitments to calculate the resulting DSR.

6. Estimated car budget

The final result shows an estimated maximum car price, monthly instalment, down payment, loan amount, interest and upfront cash.

ABOUT

Malaysia Car Affordability Calculator

The Malaysia Car Affordability Calculator is designed to help you estimate a practical car budget using simple income, commitment and financing assumptions.

Instead of looking only at the advertised car price, the calculator considers your existing commitments, target DSR, down payment, interest rate and loan tenure.

The result is an estimate rather than a guarantee of loan approval. Different banks and financial institutions may use different affordability assessments and lending criteria.
