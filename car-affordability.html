HOW IT WORKS

How car affordability is estimated

1. Monthly income

The calculator starts with your monthly gross income. This is used to estimate the amount of debt payments that may fit within your selected DSR target.

2. Existing commitments

Existing loans and regular debt commitments reduce the amount available for a new car instalment.

3. DSR

Debt Service Ratio (DSR) compares monthly debt commitments with income. A lower target DSR gives you a more conservative estimate.

4. Loan amount

The calculator estimates the loan amount that could correspond to the monthly payment available under your selected assumptions.

5. Interest and tenure

Your estimated interest or profit rate and loan tenure affect the monthly instalment and total repayment.

6. Final DSR

The calculator also shows the estimated DSR after adding the selected car's monthly instalment to your existing commitments.
