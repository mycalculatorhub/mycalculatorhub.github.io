How Car Affordability Is Estimated

What is DSR?

Debt Service Ratio (DSR) compares your monthly debt commitments with your income. For example, a 40% target DSR means total monthly debt payments are targeted at around 40% of your monthly income.

Why does the calculator use existing commitments?

Your existing loans and debt payments reduce the amount available for a new car instalment. The calculator first estimates how much monthly debt you can support, then subtracts your existing commitments.

What does the interest rate mean?

Enter the estimated rate offered by your bank or financing provider. The result is an illustration and should not be treated as a bank quotation.

Why is the actual bank result different?

Banks may consider factors such as income stability, existing debt, credit history, vehicle type, vehicle age, financing margin, employment profile and their internal affordability assessment.

New vs used cars

Financing margins can differ between new and used vehicles. For example, some banks advertise up to 90% financing for eligible new vehicles, while used-vehicle margins may vary according to the vehicle and applicant.
