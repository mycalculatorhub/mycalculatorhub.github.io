HOW IT WORKS

How the EIS calculator works

1. Enter your wages

Enter your monthly wages. For contribution purposes, wages above RM6,000 use the statutory RM6,000 ceiling.

2. Check age eligibility

Employees aged 18 to 60 are generally required to contribute. Employees aged 57 and above with no prior EIS contributions before age 57 are exempt.

3. Employee contribution

The employee share is 0.2%, with the actual deduction determined by the statutory Second Schedule contribution table.

4. Employer contribution

The employer pays an equal 0.2% share, using the same statutory contribution schedule.

5. Wage ceiling

The EIS contribution ceiling is RM6,000. The maximum employee and employer contribution is RM11.90 each per month.

6. EIS is not PCB

EIS is a social insurance contribution, not an income-tax deduction. It is separate from EPF, SOCSO and PCB.

ABOUT

Malaysia EIS Calculator

The Employment Insurance System (EIS), also known as SIP or LINDUNG KERJAYA, is administered by PERKESO under the Employment Insurance System Act 2017 (Act 800).

PERKESO states that private-sector employees aged 18 to 60 are generally covered, subject to the statutory exceptions. Contributions are shared equally between employee and employer.

We are not affiliated with PERKESO or any Malaysian government agency.
