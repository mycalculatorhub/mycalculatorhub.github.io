How Malaysia EIS Works

The Employment Insurance System (EIS) is administered by PERKESO under Malaysia's Employment Insurance System Act 2017.

For eligible employees, both the employee and employer contribute to EIS each month.

  • Employee contribution: 0.2% based on the statutory contribution schedule.
  • Employer contribution: 0.2% based on the statutory contribution schedule.
  • Total contribution: 0.4%.
  • The contribution wage ceiling is RM6,000 per month.
  • For wages above RM6,000, the contribution remains capped at the RM6,000 rate.

EIS contributions are calculated using the official statutory contribution schedule rather than simply multiplying the exact salary by 0.2%.

About This EIS Calculator

This Malaysia EIS Calculator is designed to provide a quick estimate of Employment Insurance System contributions for eligible employees and employers.

The calculator uses the statutory EIS contribution schedule and applies the RM6,000 monthly wage ceiling.

EIS eligibility can depend on factors such as employment type and age. Permanent government employees, domestic workers and self-employed persons are generally outside the standard EIS coverage.

Employees aged 18 to 60 are generally covered, subject to the applicable statutory rules and exemptions.
