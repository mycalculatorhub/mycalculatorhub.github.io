ABOUT

About Malaysia Calculator Hub

Malaysia Calculator Hub is an independent calculator project designed to make everyday Malaysian financial, employment, household and engineering calculations easier to understand.

Our calculators are designed for quick estimates and educational or planning purposes. Where possible, calculations are based on publicly available information from relevant Malaysian government and statutory bodies.

Malaysia Calculator Hub is not affiliated with KWSP, PERKESO, HASiL or any Malaysian government agency.

PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

Malaysia Calculator Hub is designed so that calculator inputs can generally be processed directly in your browser. We do not require an account to use the calculators.

Information entered into calculators

Calculator inputs are processed locally in your browser to produce the displayed result. We do not intentionally collect or store salary or other calculator values on our server.
