HOW IT WORKS

What gets deducted from your salary?

EPF / KWSP

Your employee retirement contribution. The calculator follows the current contribution-rate structure and wage-range approach for applicable Malaysian/PR employees.

SOCSO / PERKESO

Employment-related social protection contribution calculated using the current PERKESO contribution schedule and RM6,000 wage ceiling.

LINDUNG 24 Jam

An additional non-employment injury protection contribution. Local participation is voluntary; foreign-worker treatment is different.

EIS

Employment Insurance System contribution paid by both employee and employer, subject to the current wage ceiling and eligibility rules.

PCB

Monthly tax deduction. Our public calculator provides an estimate using current Malaysian tax rates and common relief assumptions; payroll PCB can differ.

Take-home pay

Gross salary minus employee EPF, SOCSO, LINDUNG 24 Jam, EIS and estimated PCB.

ABOUT

About Malaysia Calculator Hub

Malaysia Calculator Hub is an independent calculator project designed to make everyday Malaysian financial, employment and practical calculations easier to understand. Our calculators are built for quick estimates and educational use.

We aim to build calculators covering Malaysian employment and personal finance as well as practical engineering and manufacturing calculations.

We use publicly available information from Malaysian government and statutory bodies where possible, including KWSP, PERKESO and HASiL. Rules and contribution schedules can change, so users should confirm important payroll or tax figures with their employer, KWSP, PERKESO or HASiL.

We are not affiliated with KWSP, PERKESO, HASiL or any Malaysian government agency.
