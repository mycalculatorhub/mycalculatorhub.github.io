How the Working Days Calculator Works

This calculator counts the number of days from the selected start date to the selected end date, including both dates. When the weekend option is selected, Saturdays and Sundays are excluded from the working-day count.

Example:

If you select Monday as the start date and Friday as the end date, the calculator counts 5 working days when weekends are excluded.

Working Days vs Calendar Days

Calendar days include every day between the two dates, including Saturdays and Sundays. Working days normally exclude weekends and may also exclude public holidays depending on your work schedule.

Public Holidays

Public holidays can vary depending on the state, employment arrangement and holiday calendar. For this reason, this version does not automatically remove Malaysian public holidays. You can use the working-day result as a base calculation and adjust it for applicable public holidays.
