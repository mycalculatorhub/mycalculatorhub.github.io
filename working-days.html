HOW IT WORKS

How the Working Days Calculator works

This calculator counts the number of calendar days between the selected start and end dates, including both dates. When the weekend option is selected, Saturdays and Sundays are excluded from the working-day count.

📅 Calendar days

Calendar days include every day from the start date through the end date, including weekends.

💼 Working days

When weekends are excluded, Saturdays and Sundays are removed from the working-day calculation.

⏱️ Working hours

Estimated working hours are calculated by multiplying working days by your selected working hours per day.

📊 Working weeks

Estimated working weeks are calculated by dividing working days by 5.

🗓️ Public holidays

Public holidays are not automatically removed because applicable holidays can differ by state and employer.

🔢 Example

A Monday-to-Friday period contains 5 working days when Saturdays and Sundays are excluded.
