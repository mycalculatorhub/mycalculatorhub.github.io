HOW IT WORKS

How Malaysia EPF contributions are calculated

1. Monthly wages

Enter your monthly wages before EPF deductions. The calculator uses your wages to determine the applicable KWSP contribution range.

2. Age

EPF contribution rates differ once an employee reaches age 60. The calculator uses your age to determine the applicable rules.

3. Employee category

Malaysian citizens, Permanent Residents and certain non-Malaysian employees are subject to different statutory contribution requirements.

4. Wage range

For applicable Malaysian and PR employees with wages up to RM20,000, contributions are based on the statutory Third Schedule wage-range approach.

5. Employee contribution

The employee contribution is deducted from the employee's wages and paid into the EPF account.

6. Employer contribution

The employer contribution is an additional amount paid by the employer into the employee's EPF account.

CURRENT RATES

EPF contribution rules

Malaysian / PR below age 60

Employee contribution: 11%

Employer contribution: 13% for monthly wages of RM5,000 and below, or 12% above RM5,000.

Malaysian age 60+

Employee contribution: 0%

Employer contribution: 4%.

PR age 60+

Employee contribution: 5.5%

Employer contribution: 6.5% for wages of RM5,000 and below, or 6% above RM5,000.

Current non-Malaysian category

Employee contribution: 2%

Employer contribution: 2%.

The current mandatory contribution framework took effect from the October 2025 salary/wage period. KWSP states that contributions are generally based on the Third Schedule, with direct percentage calculations permitted for wages above RM20,000.

ABOUT

About the Malaysia EPF Calculator

The Malaysia EPF Calculator is designed to provide a convenient estimate of statutory EPF contributions for employees and employers in Malaysia.

The calculator uses the current contribution framework published by the Employees Provident Fund (KWSP), including the Third Schedule wage-range approach for applicable salaries up to RM20,000.

Contribution rules can change when legislation, schedules or official KWSP announcements are updated. Users should verify important payroll calculations against the latest official KWSP information.

Official source: KWSP EPF Act 1991 – Third Schedule
