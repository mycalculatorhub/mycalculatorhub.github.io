HOW IT WORKS

How Malaysia EPF contributions are calculated

1. Monthly wages

Enter your monthly wages before EPF deductions. The calculator uses the amount to determine the applicable KWSP contribution range.

2. Age

EPF contribution rates change when an employee reaches age 60. The calculator therefore uses your age to determine the applicable contribution rules.

3. Employee category

Malaysian citizens, Permanent Residents and non-Malaysian employees can have different statutory contribution requirements.

4. Wage-range calculation

For Malaysian and PR employees with wages up to RM20,000, the statutory Third Schedule wage-range approach is used rather than simply multiplying the exact salary by the contribution percentage.

ABOUT

About the Malaysia EPF Calculator

The Malaysia EPF Calculator is designed to provide a convenient estimate of statutory EPF contributions for employees and employers in Malaysia.

The calculator uses the current contribution framework published by the Employees Provident Fund (KWSP), including the Third Schedule wage-range approach for applicable salaries up to RM20,000.

Contribution rules can change when new legislation, schedules or official KWSP announcements take effect. Users should therefore verify important payroll calculations against the latest official KWSP information.

Official source: KWSP EPF Act 1991 – Third Schedule
