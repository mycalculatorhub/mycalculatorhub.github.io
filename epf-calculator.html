How EPF Contributions Work in Malaysia

EPF, also known as KWSP, is Malaysia's mandatory retirement savings scheme for eligible employees. Both employees and employers contribute to the employee's EPF account.

For Malaysian employees and permanent residents below age 60, the statutory employee contribution is generally 11%. The employer contribution is generally 13% for monthly wages of RM5,000 and below, and 12% for wages above RM5,000.

Employees aged 60 and above generally have a different statutory contribution structure. Non-Malaysian employees registered from 1 August 1998 generally use a 2% employee and 2% employer contribution rate.

Important: Actual EPF contributions for wages up to RM20,000 are determined using the official KWSP Third Schedule wage ranges. This calculator provides an estimate using the statutory contribution rates and should not be treated as an official payroll calculation.

Example

If your monthly salary is RM5,000 and you are a Malaysian employee below age 60, the statutory rates are 11% for the employee and 13% for the employer.

A simple percentage estimate would therefore be RM550 employee contribution and RM650 employer contribution, for RM1,200 total. However, the actual statutory contribution should be checked against the applicable KWSP Third Schedule wage range.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the EPF contribution rate in Malaysia?

For Malaysian employees and permanent residents below 60, the employee contribution is generally 11%. Employer contribution is generally 13% for wages up to RM5,000 and 12% above RM5,000.

Does the employer contribute to EPF?

Yes. Eligible employers are required to contribute to EPF according to the applicable statutory contribution schedule.

What happens after age 60?

EPF contribution rates change after age 60. For Malaysian employees, the employee share is generally 0% and the employer share is 4%.

Is this calculator official?

No. This is an independent calculator provided for estimation and educational purposes. For official contribution amounts, refer to KWSP's current Third Schedule and your EPF statement.
