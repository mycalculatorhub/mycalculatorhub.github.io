HOW IT WORKS

How EPF contributions work in Malaysia

Employee contribution

For Malaysian employees and permanent residents below age 60, the statutory employee contribution is generally 11%. The actual contribution amount for wages up to RM20,000 is determined using the applicable KWSP wage-range schedule.

Employer contribution

For Malaysian employees below age 60, the employer contribution is generally 13% for wages of RM5,000 and below and 12% for wages above RM5,000, subject to the official contribution schedule.

Age 60 and above

Contribution rates change after age 60. For Malaysian citizens aged 60 and above, the employee share is generally 0% and the employer share is generally 4%.

Non-Malaysian employees

Applicable non-Malaysian employees registered as EPF members from 1 August 1998 generally use a 2% employee and 2% employer contribution rate.

Wage-range calculation

For wages up to RM20,000, employers generally use the contribution amount specified in the KWSP Third Schedule rather than simply multiplying the wage by the headline percentage.

Total EPF contribution

Your total monthly EPF contribution consists of your employee contribution plus your employer's contribution.

EXAMPLE

EPF example

Suppose a Malaysian employee below age 60 earns RM5,000 per month. The applicable headline statutory rates are 11% employee and 13% employer. However, the actual contribution payable is determined using the KWSP Third Schedule wage range.

This distinction matters because KWSP states that employers should not simply calculate the contribution by multiplying the exact salary by the percentage for wages up to RM20,000.

For example, KWSP's published example shows that a wage of RM3,250 results in RM359 employee contribution and RM424 employer contribution under the wage-range approach.
