HOW IT WORKS

How SOCSO contributions are calculated

1. Monthly wages

Enter your monthly wages. PERKESO uses statutory wage bands rather than simply calculating a percentage from the exact salary.

2. RM6,000 ceiling

The current insured monthly wage ceiling is RM6,000. Salaries above RM6,000 use the RM6,000 contribution band.

3. First Category

The First Category covers Invalidity, Employment Injury and Non-Employment Injury when LINDUNG 24 JAM applies.

4. Second Category

The Second Category covers Employment Injury and Non-Employment Injury, with the applicable employee contribution for LINDUNG 24 JAM.

5. LINDUNG 24 JAM

LINDUNG 24 JAM provides protection for eligible non-employment accidents. For local employees, participation is voluntary.

6. Employee vs employer

The calculator separates the amount deducted from the employee from the contribution paid by the employer.

LINDUNG 24 JAM

What happens if you opt out?

Malaysian / PR — Opted in

The Phase 1 LINDUNG 24 JAM contribution of 0.75% is added to the employee's contribution.

Malaysian / PR — Opted out

The LINDUNG 24 JAM contribution is RM0.00. The normal applicable SOCSO contribution continues separately.

Foreign workers

LINDUNG 24 JAM is mandatory for foreign workers. The Phase 1 employee contribution is 0.75%.

Phase 1

The current Phase 1 rate is 0.75% for the first two years. Later phases increase to 1.00% and then 1.25%.

LINDUNG 24 JAM contributions are fully borne by the employee. Employers do not pay the LINDUNG 24 JAM share.

ABOUT

About the Malaysia SOCSO Calculator

The Malaysia SOCSO Calculator provides an estimate of employee and employer contributions under the Employees' Social Security Act 1969 (Act 4).

The calculator uses the current 2026 Phase 1 contribution schedule and the RM6,000 monthly wage ceiling.

LINDUNG 24 JAM is shown separately so users can understand the difference between normal SOCSO contributions and the additional non-employment injury contribution.
