HOW IT WORKS

How SOCSO contributions are calculated

💰

1. Enter your wages

Enter your monthly wages. PERKESO contributions are subject to the RM6,000 monthly wage ceiling.

🇲🇾

2. Select employee type

Local employees and foreign workers have different contribution arrangements.

📋

3. Select category

The First Category includes invalidity and employment injury protection. The Second Category covers employment injury.

🛡️

4. LINDUNG 24 JAM

Local employees can choose whether to participate. The contribution is fully borne by the employee.

📊

5. Statutory wage bands

The calculator uses the statutory wage-band contribution amounts instead of a simple percentage approximation.

🧮

6. View the result

The calculator shows employee contribution, employer contribution and the combined monthly and annual totals.

LINDUNG 24 JAM

Optional protection for local employees

LINDUNG 24 JAM provides protection for non-employment injuries, including qualifying accidents outside working hours.

For local employees, participation is voluntary and the contribution is fully paid by the employee. The Phase 1 contribution rate is 0.75%, subject to the statutory wage ceiling.

Foreign workers are subject to mandatory non-employment injury protection under the applicable PERKESO rules.
