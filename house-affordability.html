How does the House Affordability Calculator work?

The calculator estimates your maximum affordable monthly housing payment based on your selected debt-service ratio (DSR).

It then uses your selected interest rate and loan tenure to estimate the loan amount that could be supported by that monthly payment.

Finally, the calculator adds your selected down payment assumption to estimate a potential maximum property price.

What is DSR?

DSR stands for Debt Service Ratio. It compares your monthly debt obligations with your monthly income.

For example, if your monthly income is RM5,000 and your total monthly debt payments are RM2,000:

DSR = RM2,000 ÷ RM5,000 × 100 = 40%

Important: There is no single DSR percentage that guarantees home-loan approval for every Malaysian borrower. Banks may use different criteria depending on the borrower, income, existing debts, property and loan application.

What should I include in existing commitments?

Enter your existing monthly debt repayments, such as:

  • Car loan
  • Personal loan
  • Existing housing loan
  • Other financing commitments
  • Relevant recurring credit obligations

The more accurate your commitments are, the more useful the affordability estimate will be.

Why does the interest rate matter?

A higher interest rate increases the monthly repayment for the same loan amount. This means your estimated affordable house price can decrease when the assumed interest rate increases.

Because home-loan rates can change, you can enter different interest rates to see how sensitive your affordability is.

Important information

Estimate only: This calculator is intended for general financial planning and educational purposes. It does not represent a loan approval, financial advice or a guarantee from any bank. Actual loan eligibility may depend on your credit history, income stability, age, employment status, existing commitments, bank policies, property type, loan-to-value requirements and other factors.
