HOW IT WORKS

How the house affordability calculator works

1. Income

Enter your monthly income. You can also include a spouse's monthly income when assessing a joint affordability scenario.

2. Existing commitments

Include your existing monthly debt commitments such as car loans, personal loans, credit facilities and other regular repayments.

3. Target DSR

Your target debt-service ratio determines how much of your combined income is allocated to debt repayments.

4. Loan amount

The calculator estimates the maximum loan supported by your available monthly housing payment, interest rate and loan tenure.

5. Down payment

Your selected down payment determines the estimated purchase price that can be supported by the calculated loan amount.

6. Upfront costs

The calculator also estimates selected legal fees, transfer stamp duty, loan stamp duty and other initial costs so you can see the potential cash required at the start of the purchase.

ABOUT

Malaysia House Affordability Calculator

The Malaysia House Affordability Calculator is designed to help you estimate a practical home budget using income, existing commitments and basic home-loan assumptions.

The calculator considers your target DSR, down payment, interest rate and loan tenure to estimate a maximum house price and monthly instalment.

It also provides an estimate of selected upfront costs associated with a property purchase. Actual costs can vary depending on the property, transaction structure, professional fees and applicable rules at the time of purchase.
