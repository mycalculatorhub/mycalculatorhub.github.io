How This House Affordability Calculator Works

This Malaysia house affordability calculator estimates the maximum property price you may be able to afford based on your household income, existing monthly debt commitments and selected Debt Service Ratio (DSR).

What is DSR?

Debt Service Ratio compares your monthly debt payments with your monthly income. For example, a 40% DSR means total monthly debt payments are limited to approximately 40% of the income used in the calculation.

Why do existing commitments matter?

Existing car loans, personal loans, credit-card commitments and other debts reduce the amount of monthly payment available for a new housing loan.

Why does the interest rate matter?

A higher interest rate generally means a higher monthly instalment for the same loan amount. The calculator therefore lets you change the estimated rate to see how affordability changes.

What about the down payment?

A higher down payment reduces the amount that needs to be borrowed. This can reduce the monthly instalment and the total interest paid over the loan period.

Important

This calculator provides an estimate only. Actual bank approval can depend on your age, credit profile, income stability, existing commitments, property type, property value, financing margin, valuation and the bank's own lending criteria.

Legal fees, stamp duty and other transaction costs can also vary. Always confirm the actual amount with your bank, lawyer or relevant authority before making a property purchase decision.
