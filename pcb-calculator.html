How It Works

How Malaysia PCB Is Estimated

PCB is a monthly deduction from employment income towards your estimated income tax liability.

1. Estimate Annual Income

Monthly salary is annualised and optional bonus or additional remuneration is added to estimate total employment income for the year.

2. Apply EPF & Reliefs

Employee EPF and eligible tax reliefs are taken into consideration when estimating taxable income.

3. Calculate Income Tax

The estimated chargeable income is applied against the applicable progressive individual income tax bands.

4. Apply Zakat Rebate

Entered eligible zakat payments are treated as a tax rebate in the estimate, subject to applicable Malaysian tax rules.

About This Calculator

Malaysia PCB Calculator 2026

This calculator is designed to provide a convenient estimate of monthly PCB for Malaysian employees.

Monthly Tax Deduction (PCB) is a mechanism used by employers in Malaysia to deduct income tax from employees' remuneration and remit the deduction to the Inland Revenue Board of Malaysia (HASiL).

Your actual PCB can differ from this estimate because an employer's computerized PCB calculation can incorporate accumulated remuneration, previous deductions, previous employers, additional remuneration, TP1 claims and other payroll information.

For this reason, the result should be used for planning and estimation rather than as an official tax assessment or payroll calculation.
