HOW IT WORKS

How the PCB calculator works

1. Enter your salary

Enter your monthly gross employment income and any expected annual bonus or additional remuneration.

2. Select your tax status

Resident individuals are generally taxed using progressive rates and may qualify for applicable tax reliefs and rebates.

3. Apply EPF and reliefs

The calculator considers employee EPF and the relief amounts entered by you when estimating chargeable income.

4. Calculate estimated tax

The estimated chargeable income is applied against the applicable progressive individual income-tax bands.

5. Apply zakat rebate

Eligible zakat payments can reduce tax payable through the applicable tax rebate, subject to the relevant rules.

6. Estimate monthly PCB

The estimated annual tax after applicable rebate is divided over 12 months to provide an indicative monthly PCB amount.

ABOUT

Malaysia PCB Calculator

Monthly Tax Deduction (PCB), also known as Monthly Tax Deduction or MTD, is a mechanism used by employers in Malaysia to deduct income tax from employment income and remit the deduction to the Inland Revenue Board of Malaysia (HASiL).

This calculator is designed to provide a convenient estimate based on standard employment-income information. Actual PCB can differ because the official computerized calculation may use cumulative payroll information, previous deductions, additional remuneration and other employee information.

We are not affiliated with HASiL, the Inland Revenue Board of Malaysia or any Malaysian government agency.
