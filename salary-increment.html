HOW IT WORKS

How to calculate a salary increment

A salary increment is usually expressed as a percentage of your current monthly salary. The calculator converts the percentage into a monthly increase and calculates your new monthly and annual salary.

📈 Salary increase

Your salary increase is calculated by multiplying your current monthly salary by the increment percentage.

💰 New monthly salary

Your new salary is your current salary plus the monthly salary increase.

📊 Annual salary

Monthly salary is multiplied by 12 to estimate your annual gross salary.

🧮 Example

A RM4,000 salary with a 10% increment gives a RM400 monthly increase and a new salary of RM4,400.

💼 Job offers

Use the calculator to compare a proposed salary against your current salary before accepting an offer.

📅 Annual impact

The annual increase shows the additional gross salary over 12 months before deductions.
