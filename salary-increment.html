How to Calculate a Salary Increment

A salary increment is usually expressed as a percentage of your current monthly salary. For example, if your current salary is RM4,000 and you receive a 10% increment, your salary will increase by RM400 per month.

Salary Increase
Current Salary × Increment Percentage ÷ 100

New Salary
Current Salary + Salary Increase

For example, a RM4,000 monthly salary with a 10% increment results in a RM400 monthly increase and a new monthly salary of RM4,400.

Monthly vs Annual Salary Increase

Your monthly increment can be useful when comparing job offers, promotions or annual salary reviews. The annual increase shows the additional gross salary you would receive over 12 months, before deductions such as EPF, SOCSO, EIS and PCB.
