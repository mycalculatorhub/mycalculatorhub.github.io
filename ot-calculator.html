How is Malaysian overtime calculated?

A commonly used calculation for overtime is based on the employee's ordinary rate of pay and hourly rate of pay.

For a monthly-rated employee, the ordinary daily rate is commonly calculated using the monthly salary divided by 26. The hourly rate is then calculated by dividing the daily rate by the normal working hours per day.

This calculator uses the following indicative multipliers:

  • Normal working day: 1.5× hourly rate
  • Rest day: 2.0× hourly rate
  • Public holiday: 3.0× hourly rate
Important: This calculator provides an estimate and should not be treated as legal or payroll advice. Actual overtime calculations can depend on your employment contract, working arrangements and applicable Malaysian employment law.

Example

If your monthly basic salary is RM3,000 and you work 10 hours of overtime on a normal working day:

Daily rate = RM3,000 ÷ 26 = RM115.38

Hourly rate = RM115.38 ÷ 8 = RM14.42

OT rate = RM14.42 × 1.5 = RM21.63 per hour

10 hours OT = approximately RM216.35
