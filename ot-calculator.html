HOW IT WORKS

How the Malaysia OT calculator works

1. Monthly salary

Enter your monthly salary. The calculator uses this amount to estimate the ordinary rate of pay.

2. Ordinary rate of pay

For a monthly-rated employee, the calculator estimates the ordinary rate of pay by dividing monthly wages by 26.

3. Hourly rate

The estimated ordinary rate of pay is divided by your normal working hours per day to calculate the hourly rate of pay.

4. Normal working day

Overtime beyond normal working hours on a normal working day is calculated at a minimum rate of 1.5 times the hourly rate of pay under the Employment Act 1955.

5. Rest day

The calculator uses a 2.0× hourly-rate assumption for overtime hours worked on a rest day.

6. Public holiday

Overtime performed beyond normal working hours on a paid public holiday is calculated using a 3.0× hourly-rate assumption.

ABOUT

Malaysia OT Calculator

The Malaysia OT Calculator is designed to provide a quick estimate of overtime pay for monthly-rated employees using their salary, normal working hours and overtime hours.

The calculator separates overtime into normal working days, rest days and public holidays because different statutory minimum rates can apply.

The calculator is intended for general planning and estimation. Actual payroll calculations can depend on the employee's category, contract, working arrangement and applicable legislation.

DISCLAIMER

OT Calculator Disclaimer

This calculator is provided for general information and personal payroll planning purposes only. The results are estimates and should not be treated as legal or payroll advice.

JTKSM states that employees earning more than RM4,000 per month, except manual employees, generally are not eligible to claim statutory overtime, rest-day and paid-holiday payments under the Employment Act 1955.

The Employment Act 1955 applies to Peninsular Malaysia and the Federal Territory of Labuan. Sabah and Sarawak have separate labour ordinances. Always confirm your entitlement and actual payroll calculation with your employer or the relevant labour authority.
